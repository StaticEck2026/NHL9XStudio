Animation Builder

Pose the skeleton of a retail skater, get all eight facings, build a sequence. Every pixel is the game's own.

The sprite library is not built yet

The builder works on the games' own frames, built from your own copies. Put the retail NHLPA '93 and NHL '94 Genesis ROMs and the NHL 95 PC CD image (.iso, .img or .mdf) in roms/, then run:

node tools/build-sprite-layers.js
node server.js

and reload this page. See docs/sprite-library/README.md.

Drag a hand, elbow, skate, knee or the stick's ends: the limb is swapped for the retail limb of this facing that best matches. Drag a shoulder, hip or the head to move that part.
